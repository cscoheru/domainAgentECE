--- observation (not a deviation): the `org:org:` double prefix ---
TASK §5 step 1.3 specifies org ids literally as 'org:consulting-a'/'org:consulting-b'.
The scope object_ref format is 'org:<org_id>', so it renders as 'org:org:consulting-a'.
Cosmetic only: nothing parses the prefix, and consistency is what matters for
matching. Documented rather than silently renamed, because the org id value itself
is fixed by the task book.
