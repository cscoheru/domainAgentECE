== full message ==
feat(OEI-008): thread caller identity through ContentEnginePort

Give every engine call a record of *who* asked, and stop mirroring the
engine selector by hand.

- port.py: EngineCallerContext (frozen; user_ref/roles/department/
  is_management/org_id/source) + `caller` on all five Port methods +
  an `engine_name` Protocol property. port.py still imports nothing
  from Onyx/JWT/DB.
- caller.py (new): caller_from_request_headers() — cheap, header/JWT
  only, for read paths; caller_from_db_identity() — resolves the full
  Identity through ece.identity.parser, for the write path.
- adapters: `engine_name`, and one `_audit(what, *, caller, result)`
  row per call on every exit path. Uploads refuse explicitly-anonymous
  callers before any HTTP leaves the box.
- engine_merge.py: drop `_engine_switch_is_onyx()` and the
  `os.environ.get("ECE_CONTENT_ENGINE")` mirror; dispatch on
  `engine.engine_name` instead.
- 4 call sites wired: /library, /engine/status, POST /documents (403
  for anonymous), GET /documents/{id}.
- docs/API.md §13: the contract, the per-endpoint anonymous policy,
  and the known limitation that the CE cannot enforce permissions
  (retrieval is not filtered by caller — that is OEI-009).

Two real defects surfaced while capturing evidence, both in the live
adapter's audit trail: `engine_status`'s ok-row was appended after its
`return` (dead code, so a successful status call left no identity
record), and `search`'s success path had no audit row at all. Both
fixed; failure branches that were missing rows were filled in to match.

Suite: 757 passed / 0 failed (was 737; +20 new identity tests).
pyproject.toml and uv.lock unchanged.
